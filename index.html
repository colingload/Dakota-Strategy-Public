For owners thinking about the exit

Dakota Strategy

Strategic Advisory

I work with trades business owners approaching the end of their run. Owners who are not ready to sell tomorrow, but who are thinking seriously about what the business needs to look like before they can exit on their terms.

I work at the ownership layer across Corporate, Business, and Functional strategy. I model what the business is worth today, what it could be worth, and what it will take to close that gap. Much of this is reducing owner dependency and moving from an SDE business to an EBITDA business.
